# Draft handoff checklist

Tie the handoff to the design actually reviewed:

- source brief and its provenance; requirements/code revision inspected;
- exact destination file, page, frame IDs, viewport/state and durable screenshots;
- responsive rules, content limits, hierarchy, typography, semantic tokens and component identities;
- imported canonical resources versus labelled local equivalents, asset sources/crops and fallbacks;
- entry/destination/dismissal/action/recovery semantics; existing versus DESIGN-ONLY capability;
- accessibility measurements, implementation requirements and unverified keyboard/focus/reading-order behaviour;
- defects found, revisions made and before/after evidence;
- actual structure/render/prototype checks, separately from catalog availability or wiring;
- dependencies, unresolved decisions and the active project's approval status.

Retain a small current-node ledger and supersession links for substantial work. Do not infer that a historical source approval covers the new draft. Engineering should build from the exact accepted revision, under the target repository's implementation conventions.
